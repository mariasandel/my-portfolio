# Discussion Guide

Source: the four transcripts in `01 Discover/Primary Research/Raw` (P01 Lena, P02 Tobias, P03 Aiyana, P04 Jonas) · Date: 2026-10-19

This is a **record** of the guide the four interviews actually followed. It was rebuilt after the fact from the interviewer's questions. It is not a plan for new sessions. Questions are quoted from the transcripts where possible, with the participant in brackets. Anything the transcripts do not show is marked `(not in transcripts)`.

## 3. Warm-up: coffee in a normal week (about 3 min)

Every session opened with ordinary coffee habits, before any mention of the pumpkin spice latte.

- "To start, can you tell me a bit about your coffee habits during a normal week?" (Lena) · "…how coffee fits into your week?" (Jonas)
- "Thinking about a normal working week, how does coffee usually fit into it?" (Aiyana)
- "When you do go out, where do you go?" (Tobias)

Note their first description of their habit: it comes back in the wrap-up (section 7).

## 4. The last search: a specific moment (about 10 min)

Anchor the session in one real attempt, then walk through it step by step.

- "When was the last time you looked for a pumpkin spice latte? Can you walk me through it?" (Lena, Tobias, Jonas, Aiyana)
- "Walk me through what happened that day, from the moment you left the flat." (Aiyana)
- "How did you go about finding it?" (Jonas) · "Tell me about the day you found it this year." (Lena)
- Follow their count or sequence: "You mentioned four places, and you've told me about two of them. What happened at the third one?" (Aiyana)

## 5. At the counter: contents, price, decision (about 10 min)

- "What happened when you went in and ordered?" (Lena) · "What happened once you were there?" (Jonas) · "What happened when you got there?" → "What happened next?" (Tobias)
- **Follow the question they asked:** "What did you do with that question?" (Jonas) · "Did you talk to anyone in the shop about it?" (Jonas)
- **Price, asked open:** "Do you remember what you paid for it?" (Lena) · "How did you feel about what you paid for it?" (Aiyana) · "What do you know about what it costs?" (Jonas)
- **Value:** "How do you decide whether a coffee is worth what it costs?" (Tobias) · "What would make it feel worth it to you?" (Lena)
- **What came after:** "Have you had it again since then?" (Lena) · "What did you do after you left?" (Tobias) · "Once you had it in your hand, what did you do with it?" (Aiyana)

## 6. Meaning and what would change it (about 5 min)

- "What makes this drink worth that kind of afternoon for you?" (Aiyana) · "Where did you first have one?" (Lena)
- "If you think about next autumn, what would change whether you buy it?" (Lena)
- "What would have to be different for you to order it?" (Jonas) · "What would make you buy one in a café again?" (Tobias)
- "If you could change one thing about how it went, what would have made it different for you?" (Aiyana)

## 7. Wrap-up: return to the opening (about 2 min)

Every session ended by holding the participant's first answer up against what they said later:

- "At the start you said coffee is something you pick up on the way to work. Is the pumpkin spice latte part of that too?" (Lena)
- "At the start you said you go out for coffee two or three times a week. Has any of this changed that?" (Tobias)
- "At the very start you said you don't think much about your weekday coffee beyond grabbing it. Is this one different, and if so, how?" (Aiyana)
- "At the start you said you mostly go to whichever chain is next to the U-Bahn. … is that still how you'd describe it?" (Jonas)

## 9. Interviewer notes

**Techniques that worked:**

- **Follow their own count or sequence.** Aiyana's "four places" became the structure of the session.
- **Ask what they did with a question, not what they think.** Jonas answered by opening the app and showing the ingredient list.
- **Ask about price openly.** "What do you know about what it costs?" let Jonas show it didn't matter to him, and Tobias show it was the deciding factor.
- **Use the participant's own phone.** It turns opinion into evidence (Jonas, Tobias).

**To handle on purpose next time:**

- Price came up in every session, but dietary needs only when the participant raised them (Aiyana, Jonas). The open question "Do people with dietary needs skip seasonal drinks altogether, or just ask?" was never asked directly.
- Lena's memories of Chicago were not followed up ("Where did you first have one?" was the only prompt). Nostalgia stays a weak signal (P01-O15, W).
