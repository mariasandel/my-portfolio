## Solution leaks most likely to bias the interviews

- **"Which cafés have it this season":** the focus area already implies a list or map (#19).
- **"Ingredients … allergens":** it frames trust as a missing ingredient list (#20).
- **"What you get for €6.50":** it frames price as a value-explanation problem (#21).
- **"Mobile-first service":** it makes it a screen problem before any research (#17).
