# Platform Constraints

Source: Kiezkarte (client) · Project: Buying a Pumpkin Spice Latte in Berlin · Date: 2026-10-23

## Product

- A mobile-first service for coffee buyers in Berlin.
- Partners are **independent cafés only**. No chains.

## Constraints

- **Cafés:** Small teams. Staff cannot take on extra steps during the morning rush.
- **Data:** Ingredient and availability information has to come from the cafés themselves.
- **Payments:** Cafés use different till systems. No integration with any one of them.
- **Season:** Must be live by September 2027, before the next pumpkin season.
- **Languages:** German and English.
- **Privacy:** GDPR applies. No location tracking without consent.
