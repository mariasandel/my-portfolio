# Moodboard: Look and Feel

Source: images collected by the user (Pinterest / Instagram, third-party work; for internal reference only) · Project: Buying a Pumpkin Spice Latte in Berlin · Date: 2026-10-29

Inspiration for the look and feel of the interface. It has not been applied to `03_Prototype_Mobile.html` yet. Images are in `03 Develop/Inspiration/`.

## Two roles

- **Colours and branding:** images 3, 4 and 9 (`Honey-Peach`, `Apricot-Slices`, `Pumpkin-Toggle`), chosen by the user. These set the palette and the brand voice.
- **Interface and look and feel:** all other images. These set layout, components, type and mood.

## Colours and branding

Taken from images 3, 4 and 9. The hex values are estimated from the images.

| Role | Colour | From |
|------|--------|------|
| Primary: pumpkin orange | `#E8701A` | Pumpkins, toggle track (9) |
| Bright: apricot | `#F39A2B` | Apricot flesh (4) |
| Warm accent: honey amber | `#D98A1E` | Honey stream (3) |
| Deep: burnt orange / rust | `#A8401A` | Shadows of the peach and pumpkins (3, 9) |
| Soft: peach blush | `#F2B38A` | Peach skin (3) |
| Light: cream | `#FFF6EA` | Headline type, toggle knob (9) |
| Text on light | `#3E2A20` (deep brown) | Not in these three, from the mango card (6) |

- **Brand feel:**
  - Saturated and warm, almost monochrome orange with cream type on top. Golden-hour light, juicy and glossy.
  - The season is the star. Cream on orange is the signature pairing.
- **Brand type:** heavy, condensed, all-caps display for big seasonal moments ("PUMPKIN SPICE", image 9), with a friendly rounded sans for short lines ("Pumpkin spice season"). The other images add a serif option for calm screens.
- **Signature element:** the big orange toggle with a cream knob (9), as the "it's back / season is on" symbol. It fits the season alert screen.
- **Photography:** full-bleed, close-up, top-down or macro, filling the frame with one ingredient (pumpkins, apricots, honey).
- **Watch out:** the orange-on-orange contrast is strong, but it can't be used for small text or for the safe / not safe signals. Keep those on cream with green and red.

## Direction drawn from the set

- **Mood:** warm, autumnal, tactile, a little indulgent, but calm and clean. It should feel like a treat, which matches Lena's "it has to feel like a treat, not syrup water" (P01-O13).
- **Colour:**
  - Base: cream / off-white (about `#F7F3EC`) and soft warm grey.
  - Main accents: pumpkin orange (about `#E8701A`) and honey amber (about `#D9921E`).
  - Depth: deep brown for buttons and text (about `#3E2A20`), like the mango card.
  - Keep green and red for safe / not safe only, so they stay readable as signals.
- **Type:**
  - Headlines: an elegant serif (images 1, 8) for calm screens, or a heavy condensed sans for the seasonal "it's back" moment (image 9).
  - Body: a clean, neutral sans.
- **Imagery:** close-up, warm, real food photography. Pumpkin, spices and foam, ideally from the cafés themselves.
- **Components:**
  - Drink cards: photo on top, name, price pill and ingredient tags (images 6, 7).
  - Sweetness as a scale of colour steps (image 8, "level").
  - The season alert as a big, playful toggle (image 9).
  - Rounded corners and soft shadows; one strong, full-width primary button.

## Open points

- The set leans towards product and ordering apps ("Add to cart", "Order Now"). Ordering and payments are out of scope (brief), so take the visual style only, not those actions.
- Bold, full-bleed photos must not hide the key facts: safe or not, price, real pumpkin. Fidelity is low to mid (brief), so photos can stay placeholders at first.
