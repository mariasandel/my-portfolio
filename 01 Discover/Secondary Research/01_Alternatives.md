# Alternatives and Analogous Domains

Secondary research: desk research on current alternatives (as named in the four interviews, `01 Discover/Primary Research/Processed`) and analogous domains · Project: Buying a Pumpkin Spice Latte in Berlin · Date: 2026-10-23

## What buyers use today

| Alternative | What it solves | What it misses |
|---|---|---|
| Starbucks app | Availability, ingredients, ordering ahead | Chains only |
| Instagram | Finding independent cafés that sell it | Out of date, no ingredients |
| Google Maps | Café locations and opening hours | Nothing about seasonal drinks |
| Asking the barista | Direct answer, in theory | Staff often don't know |
| Making it at home | Price and real ingredients | Effort, no café experience |

## Analogous domains

| Domain | How it solves a similar problem | What could transfer |
|---|---|---|
| **Restaurant allergen menus** | EU law requires allergens to be shown on request | Ingredients visible before ordering, by default |
| **Craft beer tap lists** | Bars publish what's on tap today, updated by staff | A live "what's on today" signal from the café |
| **Asparagus season stands** | Everyone knows when *Spargelzeit* starts and where the stands are | A shared, predictable start to the season |
| **Wine tasting notes** | Describe a wine before you buy the bottle | Knowing what it tastes like before paying |
| **Too Good To Go** | Surplus food at a lower price, reserved ahead | A lower-risk way to try something new |
