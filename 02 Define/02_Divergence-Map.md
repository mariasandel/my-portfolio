# Divergence Map

Source: `01 Discover/Primary Research/Processed/01_Observations_<Name>.md` (P01 Lena, P02 Tobias, P03 Aiyana, P04 Jonas), `02 Define/02_Synthesis.md` · Project: Buying a Pumpkin Spice Latte in Berlin · Date: 2026-10-20

## Where participants converge

| Dimension | What they share | Participants |
|---|---|---|
| Not knowing what's in it | Nobody can get a clear answer about the ingredients. | P01, P02, P03, P04 |
| Finding it | Hard to know where and when it is sold. | P01, P02, P03 |

## Where participants split

### Main split: what decides whether they buy

| | Safety first | Value first |
|---|---|---|
| **Participants** | P03, P04 | P01, P02 |
| **The deciding question** | "Can I drink it?" | "Is it worth €6.50?" |
| **Behaviour** | Ask about dairy or read ingredients before ordering (P03-O11, P04-O10) | Check the price, then often don't order (P01-O12, P02-O12, P02-O14) |
| **Evidence** | B2 Dairy hidden in the sauce | C1 €6.50 for a coffee |

### Secondary split: willingness to pay

- **High:** P03 would pay anything once a year (P03-O17).
- **Low:** P01 and P02 need it to feel like a treat or skip it (P01-O13, P02-O14).
- This split follows the main one. It does not create a third group.

### Minor split: where they buy

- **Chains:** P04 finds it easily at Starbucks and uses the app (P04-O05, P04-O10).
- **Independents:** P01, P02 and P03 look outside chains and struggle to find it.
- Only one participant on the chain side. Not strong enough to form a persona.

## Recommendation

**Two personas**, split along *what decides whether they buy*:

1. **Safety first** — built from P03 and P04
2. **Value first** — built from P01 and P02

## Most divergent participant

**P02 Tobias.** He shares the Value-first need but solves it outside the café: he makes the drink at home for about a euro, with real pumpkin (P02-O16, O17). He is the only participant who has stopped buying it.

## Not a persona

- **Tobias making it at home** (P02-O16, O17) is an outlier. Keep it as a note on the Value-first persona, not as a third persona.
