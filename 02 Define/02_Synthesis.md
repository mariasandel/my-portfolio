## Insights

1. **Buyers give up looking before they find it, because it appears without warning and the cafés they like refuse to sell it.** (A1, A2) · *P01, P02, P03.* Caveat: P04 says chains always have it.
2. **Buyers don't trust what they are ordering, because nobody, not even the barista, can say what is in it.** For dairy-free buyers this is a blocker, not an annoyance. (B1, B2) · *P01, P02, P03, P04.*
3. **Buyers accept €6.50 only when it feels like a treat, because a syrupy result makes the price feel like a rip-off.** (C1) · *P01, P02.* Caveat: P03 would pay anything once a year.
