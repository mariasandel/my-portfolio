# Need Statement

Source: confirmed themes and insights in `02 Define/02_Synthesis.md` · Project: Buying a Pumpkin Spice Latte in Berlin · Date: 2026-10-20

## Need statement

**Pumpkin spice latte buyers in Berlin need a way to know where it is sold and what is in it before they order, in order to buy it confident that it is safe for them and worth the price.**

## Evidence trail

| Part of the statement | Insight | Themes | Participants |
|---|---|---|---|
| know where it is sold | 1. Give up looking before they find it | A | P01, P02, P03 |
| know what is in it | 2. Don't trust what they are ordering | B | P01, P02, P03, P04 |
| safe for them | 2. Blocker for dairy-free buyers | B2 | P03, P04 |
| worth the price | 3. €6.50 only if it feels like a treat | C1 | P01, P02 |

## Who feels it most

- **P03 and P04** have dietary needs. For them, "safe for them" is the core of the need.
- **P01 and P02** are price-sensitive. For them, "worth the price" is the core of the need.

## Not included

- **C2 Tastes like home** is parked. It is not used in this statement.
- **Tobias making it at home** is an outlier, not part of the shared need.

## Counter-evidence to keep in mind

- "It's at every Starbucks. It's not hard." (P04-O05) — finding it may only be hard outside chains.
- "Once a year, I'd pay anything for it." (P03-O17) — "worth the price" may not apply to everyone.
