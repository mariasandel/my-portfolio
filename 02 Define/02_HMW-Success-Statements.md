# Selected HMW and Success Statements

Source: `02 Define/02_Need-Statement.md`, PS1 in `02 Define/02_Problem-Statements.md` and its audit `02 Define/02_Problem-Statement-Audit_PS1.md` · Project: Buying a Pumpkin Spice Latte in Berlin · Date: 2026-10-22

## Selected problem statement

PS1, reworded after the audit: it names the three things buyers can't find out (flags 2 and 6) and drops "nobody, not even the barista" (flag 1).

Buyers in Berlin can't find out whether a pumpkin spice latte is sold nearby, safe for them, or worth €6.50 until they are already at the counter. So they either give up or buy with doubt.

## Selected HMW

**How might we help buyers know what they are getting before they reach the counter?**

## Success statements

We will know we have succeeded when:

1. Buyers know whether a café near them sells it before they set off.
2. Buyers with dietary needs know whether it is safe for them without asking staff.
3. Buyers who pay €6.50 feel it was worth it.
4. Fewer buyers walk away from the counter without ordering.
