## Persona 1: Sam, Safety first

*Built from P03 Aiyana and P04 Jonas*

**Context:** Lives in Berlin and has a dietary restriction (lactose intolerance or vegan).

**Core need:** Know whether the drink is safe for them before they order.

**Key behaviours**
1. Asks about dairy before ordering any seasonal drink (P03-O11)
2. Reads the ingredient list on a chain's app before ordering (P04-O10)
3. Searches online and walks to several cafés to find it (P03-O02, P03-O05)

**Quotes**
- "Oat milk doesn't help if the sauce has condensed milk in it." (P04-O11)
- "The barista had to go check the bottle. In the back." (P03-O12)
- "Is there actual pumpkin in it, or is it just a flavour?" (P04-O09)

**Pain points**
- Staff can't say what is in the sauce (B2)
- Dairy hides in the sauce even with plant milk (B2)
- Hard to find outside chains (A1, A2)

**⚠ Flags**
- Age and job are not given: the two participants differ (29 and 31) and P04 has no occupation in the data.
- "Not price-sensitive" is supported by P03 only (P03-O17). Not included as a trait.

## Persona 2: Felix, Value first

*Built from P01 Lena and P02 Tobias*

**Context:** Lives in Berlin, prefers independent cafés, watches what he spends on coffee.

**Core need:** Know whether the drink is worth €6.50 before paying for it.

**Key behaviours**
1. Checks the price on the menu board before deciding to order (P02-O12)
2. Leaves or goes back to his usual coffee when it isn't worth it (P02-O14, P01-O12)
3. Checks café chalkboards on his commute to see if it is back (P01-O03)

**Quotes**
- "Six fifty. For a coffee. I actually laughed." (P02-O13)
- "If I'm paying that, it has to feel like a treat, not syrup water." (P01-O13)
- "The good places don't do it on principle." (P02-O07)

**Pain points**
- €6.50 for a drink that may taste like syrup (C1)
- The cafés he likes don't sell it (A2)
- Never knows when it is back (A1)

**Note:** P02 makes it at home instead (P02-O16, O17). Outlier, not a trait.

**⚠ Flags**
- "Prefers independent cafés" is direct for P02 (P02-O06) and implied for P01. Treat as partly extrapolated.
- P01's nostalgia for Chicago (P01-O15) is a W observation. Not used.
